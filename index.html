Dancing

Date: 20 July 2026

Topic: Dancing

I have loved dance since I was 3 years old. Being on stage and performing is where I am my happiest and can fully be myself. I also love to choreograph new dances and learn new styles. One of my favourite things about dancing is that it makes other people happy as well. I love seeing the smiles on everyone's faces as they watch dance performances. Another thing I love about dance is the way that is brings people of different cultures and races together and helps people understand each other.

My favourite dance styles: Modern, Tap, Hip Hop and Contemporary.
